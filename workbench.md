## What it is

cue9 is an ecosystem of bespoke apps, plugins, and hardware devices that lets DSH and users interact beyond the laptop it runs on.

It starts with a desk companion. You work on something else while DSH works, and the companion shows what it's stuck on, right in your peripheral vision.
